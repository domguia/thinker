## Page Limits and Formatting

- **Main Text**: 9 pages maximum at submission; increased to 10 pages for camera-ready after discussion phase
- **References**: Do NOT count toward page limit
- **Appendix**: Unlimited pages, do NOT count toward limit
- **Optional Statements** (do NOT count toward limit, max 1 page each):
  - Reproducibility statement (encouraged)
  - Ethics statement (optional unless work raises ethical concerns)
  - AI use statement (required; describes LLM usage if applicable)
  - Acknowledgements (excluded from page count)

**Strict enforcement**: Papers exceeding page limits face automatic desk rejection.

**LaTeX Template**: Available in `/thesis/paper/iclr_latex/iclr2027/`  
Official files: `iclr2027_conference.sty`, `iclr2027_conference.tex`, `iclr2027_conference.bst`

## Anonymity Rules

- Papers must be **fully anonymous**. Author identity revealed in main text or supplementary material results in **automatic desk rejection**.
- Related arXiv papers are permitted if cited in **third person**.
- No acknowledgements that reveal identity.
- All authors must have OpenReview profiles (profiles are anonymous during review).

---

## LLM Usage Policy

**Mandatory Disclosure Required**: Include an "AI use statement" in your submission (does NOT count toward page limits).

Authors must explicitly disclose LLM use for:
- Generating synthetic datasets
- Developing theoretical models or conceptual frameworks
- Formulating mathematical claims and proof work
- Hypothesis refinement
- Research methodology design
- Implementation work
- Data cleaning and qualitative analysis
- Result interpretation
- Code editing
- Figure creation
- Literature analysis
- Paper editing for readability

**Key Principle**: Authors are fully responsible for submission contents. Substantial falsehoods or plagiarism produced by LLMs constitutes a Code of Ethics violation and may result in desk rejection.

## Reciprocal Reviewing Obligations

**Requirement**: Every submission must have **at least one author registered as a reviewer** who commits to review at least **3 papers**.

**Qualification Criteria**: Reciprocal reviewers must have at least one accepted publication at:
- **ML venues**: ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, UAI, AISTATS, JMLR, TMLR (including "Datasets & Benchmarks")
- **NLP venues**: ACL, EMNLP, EACL, NAACL, IJCNLP-AACL, CL, TACL (including "Findings")

**Timeline**:
- All authors notified to register as reviewers after abstract deadline (Sep 18, 2026)
- Reviewer assignments issued by Sep 27, 2026
- Review writing: October 1–21, 2026
- Initial reviews released: November 5, 2026

**Enforcement & Consequences**:
- **No reciprocal reviewer registered → Automatic desk rejection** at abstract deadline
- Reviewers who submit late or low-quality reviews may have their own papers **desk rejected** by rebuttal stage if they don't improve upon Area Chair warnings
- Authors cannot access their paper's reviews during rebuttal until they complete all assigned reviews (delays review access by up to 2 days)
- Limited submission allowance: Each author may co-author at most ONE paper with no eligible reciprocal reviewer among its authors

## Anonymity and Submission Details

- **Double-blind review**: Reviewers cannot see author names; authors cannot see reviewer identities
- **Submission Platform**: OpenReview (https://openreview.net/group?id=ICLR.cc/2027/Conference)
- All co-authors must have active OpenReview profiles before submission

---

## Dual Submission and Preprint Policy

**Allowed**:
- Papers citing prior related work by the authors
- Papers previously posted on arXiv (acceptable if cited in third person)
- Papers presented at workshops

**Not Allowed**:
- Papers identical or substantially similar to previously published or accepted versions at other venues

---

## Reproducibility Requirements

**Optional** reproducibility statement (max 1 page, does NOT count toward page limit).

Should reference:
- Code and data availability
- Proofs included in appendices
- Dataset descriptions

Do NOT duplicate technical details; reference supplementary materials instead.

---

## Ethics Statement

**Optional** unless your work raises ethical concerns (human subjects, dataset releases, potentially harmful insights).

**Format**: Maximum 1 page (does NOT count toward page limit)  
**Requirement**: All authors must acknowledge adherence to the Conference Code of Ethics during submission

---

## Supplementary Materials

**Optional** but encouraged, especially for code:
- Supplementary code, text, and other files can accompany submissions
- Reviewers are encouraged but not required to review supplementary materials
- Code submission is encouraged for replicability
- Appendices can follow references with unlimited page count
